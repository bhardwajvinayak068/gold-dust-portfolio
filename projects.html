Staff evaluation platform

Built for a Kuala Lumpur group that runs façade-maintenance crews and a restaurant. In use by its staff.

Status
In use by the company's staff
Built with
React, TypeScript, Postgres
Checks
Automated tests on the scoring rules

In use

The problem

Reviews were done ad hoc, with no shared standard between a façade technician and a restaurant server, so scores could not be compared and a capable worker with unsafe habits could still come out well.

What I built

Weighted scorecards for each role, scored out of ten; a safety gate that caps the overall score at 4 when safety compliance falls below the threshold; a private profile form each person fills in on their phone, which scores them automatically; and an owner overview filtered by team.

Result

The platform is in use by the company's staff. Each submission goes straight into a database instead of files passed between people, and the scoring and safety-gate rules are covered by automated tests.

A traditional South Asian strategy game, rebuilt for Android. In testing, coming to Google Play.

Status
In testing, Android
Opponents
Four AI levels
Built with

In testing

The problem

12 Taneh, also known as Bara Tehni and Alquerque, is played across South Asia, but the apps for it are dated, flat and crowded with ads, with little help for anyone learning the rules.

What I built

A rules engine for the 25-point board, including chained captures and the mandatory-capture rule; four AI opponents from beginner to master; an interactive tutorial, daily puzzles and offline play.

Result

The Android build is in testing, with automated checks on the rules, the AI and saved games. It is being prepared for release on Google Play.
