How I work

Fail loudly, never silently

The problem you can't see is the dangerous one. I give every step a time limit, a fallback and a log, so a failure shows up before a client finds it.

Build in a margin

I test sites, workflows and games against worse cases than the brief: slow phones, bad inputs, busy days and players who try every illegal move.

Have a process that keeps breaking?

Tell me what happens today and what goes wrong. I reply within one working day.
