The Mechanical & Physical Crucible

My engineering career began with four years of intensive Mechanical Engineering coursework at Sharda University (2012–2016), focusing on systems analysis, engineering mechanics, and mathematical modeling. In high-consequence physical systems, failure is never an abstract concept—it is governed by material yield limits and mandatory factors of safety.

In September 2018, I relocated to Malaysia to join the country’s premier commercial infrastructure sector. At Mitsutegap Sdn Bhd (2018–2021), I directed structural QA/QC inspection protocols, resolving defect patterns and bridging architectural consultant blueprints with on-site subcontractor execution.

From 2021 to 2024 with WCT Construction and WCT Holdings Berhad, I served on Malaysia’s landmark Tun Razak Exchange (TRX)—a 70-acre international financial district development in Kuala Lumpur. I coordinated multidisciplinary subcontractor engineering teams, conducted structural root-cause analysis on Non-Conformance Reports (NCRs), and designed safety inductions that measurably drove down near-miss incidents.

Beginning in March 2024 at Rotol Group, I took command of high-rise commercial operations across premier developments directly under KLCC. Directing industrial rope-access and ground crews at extreme heights demands relentless discipline: through rigorous enforcement of Method Statements, Permit-to-Work systems, and HIRARC risk controls, our teams achieved zero lost-time incidents across 12 consecutive months.
