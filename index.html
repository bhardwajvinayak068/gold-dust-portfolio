Questions people ask

Who is Vinayak Bhardwaj?

I'm the founder of Virin Digital, based in Kuala Lumpur since 2018. I design and build websites, set up AI automation for businesses, make documentaries on my YouTube channel Vinayak Tech, and I'm building 12 Taneh, a mobile strategy game. I also have eight years of engineering QA/QC work behind me, which is where my habit of checking everything comes from.

What can you build for my business?

Websites that load fast on phones and send every enquiry to a person; AI agent workflows and automation that take over a manual process such as lead intake, reporting or document checks; and documentary or explainer videos built from primary sources.

How do you make sure it works?

Every project goes through the same loop: brief, build, check, ship. Websites are tested for speed, with a keyboard and a screen reader, and every form is sent end to end. AI workflows have a separate step that checks each output, sends failures to a person and logs every run.
