How a website project runs

Live

Brief and structure

Who the site is for, what they need to find, and the one thing you want them to do. The page structure and copy are agreed before design starts.

Design and build

Designed around your real photos and words, then hand-built page by page and checked on real phones as it goes.

Check and launch

Before launch every page is tested for speed, with a keyboard and a screen reader, and every form is sent end to end. Then it goes live.

Questions about websites

How much does a website cost?

It depends on the number of pages and what the site has to do, so I quote per project. Tell me what you have today and what it should do, and I reply within one working day.

Do you use WordPress or a page builder?

Usually not. I hand-build sites so pages stay fast on a phone. If you need to edit content yourself, we agree how before the build starts.

Is SEO included?

The technical part is: page titles and descriptions, structured data, a sitemap, speed and accessibility. Ongoing content writing is a separate conversation.

Can you rebuild my existing site?

Yes. I start by measuring what you have, then keep what works and rebuild what slows it down or loses enquiries.

Where do enquiries go?

To a person. Forms are wired to an inbox someone checks, with WhatsApp and email as direct alternatives on every page.

Need a site that brings in enquiries?

Tell me what happens today and what goes wrong. I reply within one working day.
